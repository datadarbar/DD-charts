Pakistan's Crypto Evolution: A Timeline

Year Key Development Observation
2018
  • SBP bars banks, PSPs and exchange companies from facilitating virtual currencies in April
 De-banking Crypto Institutional access is cut off. By removing formal banking channels, crypto activity is pushed effectively into a grey/informal zone.
2020
  • SECP directs companies/LLPs to refrain from virtual currencies
  • Position paper signals possible future regulation of Digital Asset Trading Platforms
 Regulatory Exploration A subtle pivot from a blanket "warning" to acknowledging that a regulatory framework for trading platforms might eventually be necessary.
2021
  • Sindh High Court forms a multi-agency committee comprising SBP, SECP, PTA, and FMU to assess legality of crypto
 The Legal Question The debate moves from circulars to the courts. The judiciary forces a multi-agency assessment to determine if a ban or regulation is the correct path.
2022
  • FIA scam probes increase pressure on traders
  • SBP argues for a total ban in court
 Hardline Stance AML/CFT sensitivity dominates the narrative due to FATF requirements. The state views crypto primarily as a capital flight and compliance risk.
2023
  • Finance Ministry reportedly states crypto will "never be legalized"
  • SBP hints at the CBDC/Digital Rupee track
 Defining the Alternative Public narrative hardens against private crypto, while the CBDC is positioned as the safe, state-sanctioned alternative.
2024
  • Proposals reported to enable rupee issuance in digital forms
 The CBDC Blueprint While crypto activity for retail remained in legal darkness, SBP showed intent to experiment with digital rupee as legal tender, allowing them to manage currency in both virtual and physical forms.
2025
  • Pakistan Crypto Council formed in March with Bilal Bin Saqib as CEO
  • Binance Founder, CZ, onboarded as advisor
  • Virtual Assets Ordinance 2025 enacted in July, providing a basic framework for regulation and establishing PVARA to oversee crypto
  • Bitcoin strategic reserve announced, tying surplus power to Bitcoin mining & AI data centers
  • SBP signals CBDC pilot (Soramitsu)
  • Binance and HTX given no-objection certificates for exchange operations
  • Plans to explore 'tokenisation' of up to $2 billion of assets
 Regulated Adoption A massive shift takes place, from banning to embracing. The focus turns to both creating the legislative framework to oversee adoption and exploring potential in mining by building data centers through subsidized energy.
2026
  • Reuters reports agreement with SC Financial Technologies (WLFI-linked) to explore USD stablecoin rails for cross-border payments
 The Hybrid Era The ecosystem matures into a dual approach: a state-backed CBDC for domestic sovereignty alongside regulated stablecoins for international liquidity.
